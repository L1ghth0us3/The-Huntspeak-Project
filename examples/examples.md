# Example Sentences

Worked examples with a short gloss and English. Good for checking you’re conjugating the way the lessons teach.

## Basic clauses

**Î vîsat tye.**
î v-î-s-a-t tye — I see-1.PRES animal
“I see the animal.”

**Tye nenad.**
tye n-e-n-a-d — animal move-3.PRES
“The animal moves.” (the *e* already means “it”)

**Î vîset däl tye yat wäwi.**
î v-î-s-e-t däl tye yat wäwi — I see-1.PAST.REC many animal in forest
“I saw many animals in the forest.”

**Su vusat amî bi-idwa.** [bi.idʋa]
su v-u-s-a-t am-î bi idwa — you see-2.PRES my two-ear
“You see my (two) ears.”

## Tense and aspect

**Î dæl rîsash / rîsesh / rîsâsh.**
“I fight / fought / will fight a lot.”

**Î dæl iqe rîsesh.** — “If I fought a lot.”
**Î dæl iqe rîsâsh.** — “If I will fight a lot.”
**Î dæl iqe rîsâash.** — “If I will be fighting a lot.”

## Prefix verbs

**Tî vayâl. / Tî veyâl. / Tî vâyâl.**
“We have / had / will have a meeting.”

**Î wakh varadyävu.**
“I have a young child.”

**Î wakh vabiradyävu.** — “I have two young children.”
**Î wakh vadälradyävu.** — “I have many young children.”

**Î iqe vaqash su.**
“If I love you.”

## Causatives and multiple verbs

**Î danenad tye.**
“I cause the animal to move.”

**Î mîvat su tye danenad.**
“I trust you to cause the animal to move.”

**Î mîvat tye nenad.**
“I trust the animal to move.”

**Î qîrad-ba lîba qäru.**
“I want to eat food.”

## Comparison

**Su amî qar-vu sha-shidyävu.**
“You are my best friend.”

**Kharau hâiru sha-sil-du tiau nätum.**
“This cave is darker than the night.”

**Se nenad shai-du tiau tye.**
“She moves faster than an animal.”

## Questions

**Naish su buvar?** — “How are you doing?”
**Naire su luba?** — “When are you eating?”
**Naina su kharae lube?** — “Why did you eat that?”
**Naisa sha-kharau?** — “Who is this?”
**Naish amsu she-mâish?** — “How was your sleep?”

## Clause linking

**Tî sîluav wa qîhuap shilaqar.** — “We survived and helped the tribe.”
**Î vîsat tye, rîsash.** — “I see the animal and fight.”
**Î dîqab naru tye sha-turam.** — “I am afraid because the animal is big.”
**Tye sha-turam; narudi î dîqab.** — “The animal is big; therefore I am afraid.”
**Qesha î dîqab, î rîsash.** — “Although I am afraid, I fight.”
**Iqe shiä sha-led, tye nenad.** — “If the moon is bright, the animal moves.”
**Naaq-iqe shiä sha-led, tî mîda.** — “Unless the moon is bright, we sleep.”
**Î nîtar qäru wey radyävu leba qäru.** — “I cook food so that the child eats.”
**Eshu se meda, î vîdab yasyi.** — “While she sleeps, I write a tale.”
**Avikh tî rîsash, tî lîba qäru.** — “After we fight, we eat food.”
**Tarem tî mîda, tî bîsha.** — “Before we sleep, we pray.”
**Î vîsat tye, udil rîsash.** — “I see the animal, then fight.”

## Official phrases

**Î veqash su.** — “I love you.”
**Î naaq-veqash su.** — “I hate you.”

## Flagged historical examples (do not imitate)

Kept as evidence of older notes or unformalized constructions — not models to copy. Details in `texts/tale-of-our-ancestors/notes.md` and `development/gaps.md`.

- **î vîdab tye** / **î vedab tye** as see/be-seen — wrong root for “see,” and an unsettled passive idea.
- **î vîsat tu / tu vîsat î** as active/passive pair — passive still unformalized.
- **Naiqa sha-ra-su?** / **Naish râ-kharau?** — don’t match documented question order.
- **Naire tu lubâe?** — ending doesn’t match future perfective or imperfective.
- Legend oddities: **tîkhur**, **shu-ba-melu**, **sevu'qe**, article-like **kharau sheq**, **shu-budai-si**.
