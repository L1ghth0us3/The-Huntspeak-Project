# Curated Example Sentences

Each example gives Huntspeak, a morpheme-level gloss, the translation, its source, and status. Corrections applied by project-lead decision are noted. Historically attested examples that conflict with documented grammar are preserved — flagged, not deleted — in the final section.

Source keys are the same as in `lexicon/SCHEMA.md`.

## Basic clauses

**Î vîsat tye.**
î v-î-s-a-t tye — I see-1.PRES animal
"I see the animal." *(base-notes; canon)*

**Tye nenad.**
tye n-e-n-a-d — animal move-3.PRES
"The animal moves." (the pronoun *se* is implied by the *e*) *(base-notes; canon)*

**Î vîset däl tye yat wäwi.**
î v-î-s-e-t däl tye yat wäwi — I see-1.PAST.REC many animal in forest
"I saw many animals in the forest." *(base-grammar; canon)*

**Su vusat amî bi-idwa.** [bi.idʋa]
su v-u-s-a-t am-î bi idwa — you see-2.PRES my two-ear
"You see my (two) ears." *(base-grammar; canon)*

## Tense and aspect

**Î dæl rîsash / rîsesh / rîsâsh.**
"I fight / fought / will fight a lot." *(base-grammar; canon; spelling regularized from IPA-style ɪ/ʌ to î/â)*

**Î dæl iqe rîsesh.** — "If I fought a lot."
**Î dæl iqe rîsâsh.** — "If I will fight a lot."
**Î dæl iqe rîsâash.** — "If I will be fighting a lot."
*(base-grammar; canon; the original misprinted the subject of the last two as "Tu" — corrected per D10)*

## Prefix verbs

**Tî vayâl. / Tî veyâl. / Tî vâyâl.**
tî v-a-yâl — we have-PRES meeting
"We have / had / will have a meeting." *(base-grammar; canon)*

**Î wakh varadyävu.**
î wakh v-a-radyävu — I young have-PRES-child
"I have a young child." *(base-notes; canon)*

**Î wakh vabiradyävu.** — "I have two young children."
**Î wakh vadälradyävu.** — "I have many young children." *(base-notes; canon)*

**Î iqe vaqash su.**
î iqe v-a-qäsh su — I IRR have-PRES-love you
"If I love you." *(base-notes; canon; original wrote the object as tu — corrected per D10. Stem spelling qash → see D3 note in lexicon entry qäsh)*

## Causatives and multiple verbs

**Î danenad tye.**
î d-a-nenad tye — I cause-PRES-move-3 animal
"I cause the animal to move." *(base-grammar; canon)*

**Î mîvat su tye danenad.**
î m-î-v-a-t su tye d-a-nenad — I trust-1.PRES you animal cause-PRES-move-3
"I trust you to cause the animal to move." *(base-grammar; canon; the notes' variant "dunenad" superseded per D8, "tu" → "su" per D10)*

**Î mîvat tye nenad.**
"I trust the animal to move." *(base-notes; canon)*

**Î qîrad-ba lîba qäru.**
î q-î-r-a-d=ba l-î-b-a qäru — I want-1.PRES=AUX eat-1.PRES food
"I want to eat food." *(base-grammar + D2; canon)*

## Comparison

**Su amî qar-vu sha-shidyävu.**
su am-î qar=vu sh-a=shidyävu — you my good=SUP be-PRES=friend
"You are my best friend." *(base-grammar; canon; original spelled "qaar-vu" — normalized per D3/M1)*

**Kharau hâiru sha-sil-du tiau nätum.**
"This cave is darker than the night." *(base-grammar; canon)*

**Se nenad shai-du tiau tye.**
"She moves faster than an animal." *(base-grammar; canon)*

## Questions

**Naish su buvar?** — "How are you doing?" *(base-notes; canon; corrected per D9, D10)*
**Naire su luba?** — "When are you eating?" *(base-notes; canon; tu → su per D10)*
**Naina su kharae lube?** — "Why did you eat that?" *(base-notes; canon; tu → su per D10; original "libe" adjusted to lube — the i violated the subject-vowel rule)*
**Naisa sha-kharau?** — "Who is this?" *(base-notes; canon)*
**Naish amsu she-mâish?** — "How was your sleep?" *(base-notes; canon; amtu → amsu per D10)*

## Clause linking

**Tî sîluav wa qîhuap shilaqar.** — "We survived and helped the tribe." *(legend; canon)*
**Î vîsat tye, rîsash.** — "I see the animal and fight." *(enhanced/D5; canon)*
**Î dîqab naru tye sha-turam.** — "I am afraid because the animal is big." *(enhanced/D5; canon)*
**Tye sha-turam; narudi î dîqab.** — "The animal is big; therefore I am afraid." *(enhanced/D5; canon)*
**Qesha î dîqab, î rîsash.** — "Although I am afraid, I fight." *(enhanced/D5; canon)*
**Iqe shiä sha-led, tye nenad.** — "If the moon is bright, the animal moves." *(enhanced/D5; canon)*
**Naaq-iqe shiä sha-led, tî mîda.** — "Unless the moon is bright, we sleep." *(enhanced/D5; canon)*
**Î nîtar qäru wey radyävu leba qäru.** — "I cook food so that the child eats." *(enhanced/D5; canon)*
**Eshu se meda, î vîdab yasyi.** — "While she sleeps, I write a tale." *(enhanced/D5; canon)*
**Avikh tî rîsash, tî lîba qäru.** — "After we fight, we eat food." *(enhanced/D5; canon)*
**Tarem tî mîda, tî bîsha.** — "Before we sleep, we pray." *(enhanced/D5; canon)*
**Î vîsat tye, udil rîsash.** — "I see the animal, then fight." *(enhanced/D5; canon)*

## Flagged historical examples (do not imitate)

Preserved as evidence; each conflicts with documented grammar and awaits a ruling or documents an unformalized construction.

- **î vîdab tye** glossed "I see the animal" / **î vedab tye** "I am seen by the animal" *(base-notes)* — uses the root of "write" (v-d-b) for "see" (v-s-t); ruled erroneous (D10). The pair also implies an undocumented passive mechanism.
- **î vîsat tu / tu vîsat î** "I see you / you are seen by me" *(base-notes)* — correct roots; implies passive-by-vowel, still unformalized (see `development/gaps.md`).
- **Naiqa sha-ra-su?** "What are you doing?" *(base-notes)* — the sha-ra- stacking and postposed subject match no documented rule.
- **Naish râ-kharau?** "How will you do this?" *(base-notes)* — subject omitted in a 2nd-person question; undocumented.
- **Naire tu lubâe?** "When will you be eating?" *(base-notes)* — the ending -âe matches neither future perfective (-â) nor future imperfective (-âa).
- Several legend forms: **tîkhur** (subject vowel mismatch), **shu-ba-melu** (undocumented use of -ba), **sevu'qe** (imperative suffix as "told"), **kharau sheq** ("the cold" — article-like kharau), **shu-budai-si neruv** (adverbial -si + shu- progressive). See `texts/tale-of-our-ancestors/notes.md`.
