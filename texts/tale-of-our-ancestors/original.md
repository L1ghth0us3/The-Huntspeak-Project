## English

> The tale of our Ancestors
>
> Long ago our ancestors lived in small, queen-less groups. They fought the cold, thirst and hunger everyday but we were strong already. We survived and helped the tribe. But the many moons became cold. Colder and colder with every new moon. The earth and rivers were frozen over, the animals were hiding from the deadly cold. We had nothing to harvest, nothing to hunt. One of the many mothers had the name Zaiya. She feared for her tribe that was slowly starving and freezing. One night on a cold but clear-skied night she looked up at the full (holy) moon and prayed to Menphina. Menphina talked to Zaiya in her mind and told her "trust the moon and make a journey with your tribe." This journey would test their limits even more and send them through ice cold with no shelter over a large sheet of ice. Walking for days.

## Huntspeak

> Yasyi valir akhtî khash.
>
> Shrala'i akhtî khash tîkhur yat rad, naaq-kin haqar. Te resush kharau sheq, rav'i wa kik'et yad ädi ara tî shua-tir shurin. Tî sîluav wa qîhuap shilaqar. Ara däl shiä shudu-rav'i. Rav'i-du wa rav'i-du inas yaqa shiqar shiä. Weid wa sathen shu-q'rysh, tye shu-ba-melu uan ikhel-si rav'i. Tî vu-yething bîqut, vu-khu-yething. U valir däl qäin vu-wâmal Zaiya. Se dequb valir amse nayal shu-budai-si neruv wa qerush. Laya sheq ara yethieq nätum se vesut asu ada mithra shiä wa beshu ayu Menphina. Menphina sevu ayu Zaiya yat amse näli wa sevu'qe "muvat yat shiä wa ra-shiroth inas amsu shilaqar."
