# The Tale of Our Ancestors — Analysis Notes

Line-by-line commentary on the revised text. **Flagged** items are forms that documented grammar does not (yet) explain; they are preserved in the text and tracked in `development/gaps.md`. This tale is the richest specimen of connected Huntspeak and several of its constructions are the only evidence for grammar not covered in the docs.

## Sentence by sentence

**Yasyi valir akhtî khash.** — "The tale of our ancestors."
`valir` "of" + possessive `akhtî` "our". Fully regular.

**Shrala'i akhtî khash tîkhur yat rad, naaq-qin haqar.** — "Long ago our ancestors lived in small, queen-less groups."
`tîkhur` = t-kh-r "live" with V₂ = u (past long ✓). **Flagged:** V₁ is î, but the subject (*khash*, third person) predicts e (`tekhur`); the î may have been copied from `akhtî` "our". No ruling yet — do not imitate. `naaq-qin` shows the privative (grammar ch. 4).

**Te resush kharau sheq, rav'i wa qik'et yad ädi ara tî shua-yir shurin.** — "They fought the cold, thirst and hunger every day, but we were strong already."
`resush` = r-s-sh "fight", e (they) + u (past long) — fully regular. **Flagged:** `kharau sheq` uses "this" where English has "the" (article-like use, attested only here). `shua-yir` = copula sh- + ua (past-long imperfective) + "strong" — a nice attestation of the imperfective on a prefix verb.

**Tî sîluav wa qîhuap shilaqar.** — "We survived and helped the tribe."
s-l-v and q-h-p, both î (we) + ua (past long imperfective). Textbook coordination with shared subject (ch. 10).

**Ara däl shiä shudu-sheq.** — "But the many moons became cold."
`shudu-` = sh-d- "become", both vowels u = past long (ch. 4 ✓).

**Sheq-du wa sheq-du inas yaqa shiqar shiä.** — "Colder and colder with every new moon."
Comparative reduplication (ch. 6).

**Weid wa sathen shu-q'rysh, tye shu-ba-melu uan ikhel-si sheq.** — "The earth and rivers were frozen over; the animals were hiding from the deadly cold."
`shu-q'rysh` = "were frozen": copula (past long) + participle-like `q'rysh`. **Flagged:** `shu-ba-melu` "were hiding" uses `-ba` in a way the auxiliary rule doesn't describe, and this `shu- + verb` pattern competes with the documented imperfective infix (-ua-) as a progressive strategy. `ikhel-si` "deadly" = -si derivation (ch. 6 ✓).

**Tî vu-yethin bîqut, vu-khu-yethin.** — "We had nothing to harvest, nothing to hunt."
`vu-` = "have" (past long) + `yethin` "nothing"; `bîqut` = b-q-t "harvest" (î + u ✓); `vu-khu-yethin` stacks "have" + "hunt" (both past long). Elegant — and the stacking pattern is only attested here.

**U valir däl qäin vu-wâmal Zaiya.** — "One of the many mothers had the name Zaiya."
Partitive `u valir däl` "one of the many" (ch. 5).

**Se dequb valir amse shilaqar shu-budai-si neruv wa qerush.** — "She feared for her tribe, which was slowly starving and freezing."
`dequb` = d-q-b "fear" (e + u ✓); `valir` here = "for". **Flagged:** the relative clause "that was slowly starving…" is plain juxtaposition (no relativizer); `budai-si` uses -si on an adjective to make an adverb ("slowly") — both undocumented but plausible. `neruv` = n-r-v "starve" ✓, `qerush` = q-r-sh "freeze" ✓.

**Laya sheq ara yethieq nätum se vesut asu ada mithra shiä wa beshu ayu Menphina.** — "On a cold but clear-skied night she looked up at the full (holy) moon and prayed to Menphina."
`laya` "on" for time ("on a night" — small extension of the spatial sense); `vesut` = v-s-t "see/look" (e + u ✓); `asu ada` "up at"; `beshu` = b-sh- "pray" (e + u ✓); `ayu` "to(ward)" for the addressee of prayer.

**Menphina sevu ayu Zaiya yat amse näli wa sevu'qe "muvat yat shiä wa ra-shiroth inas amsu shilaqar."** — "Menphina spoke to Zaiya in her mind and told her: 'Trust the moon and make a journey with your tribe.'"
`sevu` = s-v- "speak" (e + u ✓). **Flagged:** `sevu'qe` attaches the imperative suffix to a past verb to mean "told/commanded" — the only attestation of -'qe. **Flagged:** inside the quote, `muvat` "trust!" is a command but carries u+a (ordinary present with a u subject vowel?) rather than -'qe; the imperative formation of root verbs is effectively undocumented. `ra-shiroth` = "do/make a journey" (prefix r- + present a ✓). `amsu shilaqar` "your (sg.) tribe" ✓.

## Summary of open flags in this text

| Form | Issue |
| --- | --- |
| tîkhur | subject vowel î where e is expected |
| kharau sheq | demonstrative as definite article |
| shu-ba-melu | unexplained -ba; shu- progressive vs. -ua- imperfective |
| vu-khu-yethin | stacked prefix verbs (only attestation) |
| (juxtaposed clause) | relative clauses by juxtaposition |
| budai-si | -si as adverbializer |
| sevu'qe | -'qe on a past verb = "told" |
| muvat | imperative without -'qe |
