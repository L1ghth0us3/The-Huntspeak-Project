# 7. Questions

## The question words

Content questions begin with **nai-**:

| Huntspeak | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| naiqa | what |
| nayal | which |
| naire | when |
| naido | where |
| naisa | who |
| naina | why |
| naish | how |
| naish däl | how many (literally “how many/much”) |

*Whom*, *whose*, and *whether* don’t have settled words yet — don’t invent them casually.

## Word order in questions

Put the question word **first**, keep the **subject next**, and send the **verb to the end**:

**Question word + Subject + (Object) + Verb**

> **Naish su buvar?** — “How are you (feeling)?” (the everyday greeting-question)
> **Naire su luba?** — “When are you eating?”
> **Naina su kharae lube?** — “Why did you eat that?”
> **Naisa sha-kharau?** — “Who is this?”
> **Naish amsu she-mâish?** — “How was your sleep?”

## Yes/no questions

How to *ask* a yes/no question isn’t documented yet (particle? intonation?). The answers are:

- **Qî** — yes
- **Naaq** — no
