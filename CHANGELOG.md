# Changelog

Meaningful changes to the Huntspeak language itself. Repository housekeeping is not recorded here.

## 2026-08-09 - Initial consolidation

Based on the repository audit (`development/AUDIT.md`) and project-lead decisions D1–D10 (`development/decisions/2026-08-09-initial-decisions.md`):

### Added
- Official-canon vocabulary integrated: `Shîmakh` "hello", `Râdakh` "farewell", `Shakh` (informal greeting/parting), `Qar mîdam` / `Qar nätum` / `Qar yâl` greeting formulas, `î veqash su` "I love you", `î naaq-veqash su` "I hate you".
- Verbal negation with `naaq-` documented (attested in official canon: `naaq-veqash`).
- Clause-linking system accepted as canon (D5): connectives `naru` "because", `narudi` "therefore", `qesha` "although", `tarem` "before" join the lexicon; coordination, event chaining, and subordination documented.
- Curse interjections accepted (D6): `sha` "damn (mild)", `khât` "shit/fuck (strong)".

### Changed
- "Food" is `qäru`; `evum` deprecated (D2).
- `iqe` relabeled from "hypothetical tense" to **irrealis particle** - usage unchanged (D5).
- Orthography standardized on ⟨q⟩ for /k/ (D3): `kin→qin` (in compounds), `kik'et→qik'et`, `ike→iqe`, `k-d-s→q-d-s`, `kyul'sha→qyul'sha`; stem spellings fixed to `qäsh`, `qar`.
- Root of "to starve" corrected to `n-r-v` (was `n-r-f`, containing a sound outside the inventory) (D10).
- "Nothing" standardized as `yethin` (variant `yething` deprecated) (D10).
- *The Tale of Our Ancestors*: corrected version issued fixing `rav'i`→`sheq` for "cold" (D1), `nayal`→`shilaqar` "tribe" (D4), `tir`→`yir` "strong" (D7), plus the D3/D10 spelling normalizations. Original preserved verbatim.

### Clarified (no change to the language)
- `rav'i` means "thirst" only (D1); `nayal` means "which" only (D4); "to feel" is `b-v-r` (D9); embedded prefix verbs carry the tense vowel only, never a subject vowel (`danenad`, not `dunenad`) (D8); `su`/`amsu` is the correct singular "you" in the question examples (D10); the tense description's "Future recent/long" wording corrected to "Past recent/long" (D10).

### Open questions
- D11: official canon's plural present forms (`vɪdeb` etc.) vs. the documented `a` = present rule. Repo follows `a` = present pending a ruling.
- Spelling of the strong curse: owner ruling says `khât`; the original proposal spelled it `khät`. `khât` adopted, confirmation requested.
