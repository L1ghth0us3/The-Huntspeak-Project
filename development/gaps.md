# Known Gaps

Missing or incomplete parts of Huntspeak and its documentation. **A gap is not an invitation to coin freely** - first ask whether derivation, compounding, a construction, or circumlocution covers the need (see `CONTRIBUTING.md`). Gaps are removed from this list only by project-lead-accepted material.

## Grammar

| Gap | What exists | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Verbal negation (general)** | Official canon attests `naaq-veqash` "have no love" on a prefix verb; nothing attests `naaq-` on a root verb ("I do not eat" - presumably `î naaq-lîba`, unconfirmed) | High |
| **Yes/no questions** | Nothing (particle? intonation? tag with qî/naaq?) | High |
| **Imperatives of root verbs** | `-'qe` is defined, but the only attestation is the odd `sevu'qe` "told"; the legend's actual command (`muvat`) doesn't use it | High |
| **Passive voice** | Note pair `tu vîsat î` "you are seen by me" implies a mechanism; never formalized | Medium |
| **Relative clauses** | Legend uses bare juxtaposition once | Medium |
| **Adverbs** | Comparatives in -du used adverbially; `budai-si` "slowly" (one attestation) | Medium |
| **Progressive shu- + verb** | Legend uses it thrice (incl. unexplained `shu-ba-melu`); competes with the -ua- imperfective | Medium |
| **Article-like kharau** | One legend attestation of "the" | Low |
| **Stress on prefixed verbs / compounds** | "First syllable" rule only | Low |
| **D11: plural present vowel** | See `development/decisions/` | High (blocks paradigm certainty) |

## Phonology and orthography

- **Phonotactics:** permitted clusters (`shr-`, `Cy-`), syllable shapes, diphthong inventory, undescribed.
- **Doubled vowels vs. the imperfective:** orthographic length (`naaq`) and the -aa- imperfective infix look identical; no rule distinguishes them.
- **/o/:** absent from the official vowel list; one word (`shiroth`). Marginal - avoid in new words.
- **y as a vowel:** `q'rysh`, `sylth` use y vocalically; no rule licenses it.
- **/p/ in q-h-p:** "to help" conjugates with p (`qîhuap`) though /p/ is not in the inventory.

## Lexicon (slots the sources themselves left empty)

- whom, whose, whether (interrogatives)
- to smell, to taste, to hear (perception verbs - consider derivation from yäula, yayum/qäru, idwa before coining roots)
- to lose (a fight), empty row in the original dictionary; relates to `qyul'sha` "shameful defeat"
- Curses: several slots left empty in the working notes (e.g. "By the Twelve", "By Menphina", "Moon hides from you"; some may be phrases, not words)
- **Numbers above five** - likely a design decision (base-5 compounding?) rather than five new roots
- "alone", "to disappear" (needed by proposed proverbs)

## Texts

- *The Tale of Our Ancestors*: final two English sentences untranslated ("This journey would test their limits… Walking for days.").
- Proverbs exist in English only (`development/proposals/proverbs.md`).

## Culture and usage

- Register/politeness (formal vs. informal speech; interacts with `Shakh` "informal hello" - the one attested register distinction).
- Naming conventions (personal names like Zaiya; deity names stay untranslated).
- The click/whistle hunting register (khiädyasa) is completely undescribed, official canon says it exists.
