### Proverbs / Sayings

* **“The quiet forest has teeth.”** — Calm does not mean safe.
* **“A track is a tongue.”** — Actions/evidence speak.
* **“Blood does not hide from the moon.”** — Wrongdoing leaves traces.
* **“No claw hunts alone.”** — Everyone needs others.
* **“The moon also disappears.”** — Weakness or absence does not lessen worth.
* **“Do not bare fang at your own fire.”** — Don’t turn on your own people.
* **“Far ears hear last.”** — Ignore warnings, suffer consequences.
