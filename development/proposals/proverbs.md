# Proposal: Proverbs and Sayings

Status: **proposal** - these exist only in English (from the RP cheatsheet). Each needs a Huntspeak rendering before it can even become provisional. Translating them is a good exercise for the proposal workflow; most can probably be built from existing vocabulary.

| English | Sense | Translation notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "The quiet forest has teeth." | Calm does not mean safe. | rasa wäwi + v- "have" + qän - all canon words |
| "A track is a tongue." | Actions/evidence speak. | erlä, sh- copula; "tongue" yayum is provisional |
| "Blood does not hide from the moon." | Wrongdoing leaves traces. | khela, m-l- "hide", uan shiä; needs verbal negation pattern (naaq-) |
| "No claw hunts alone." | Everyone needs others. | naaq-ayi + kh- "hunt"; "alone" has no word - gap or circumlocution |
| "The moon also disappears." | Absence does not lessen worth. | dahv "also" (provisional); "disappear" ungapped |
| "Do not bare fang at your own fire." | Don't turn on your own people. | qän, khâl; negative imperative undocumented - blocked on imperative gap |
| "Far ears hear last." | Ignore warnings, suffer consequences. | ayar idwa, nääq "last"; "to hear" is an open lexical gap |
